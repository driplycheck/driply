## 2. Data we process

From Telegram: Telegram ID, name, @username, avatar link, interface language. Entered by you: nickname, bio, gender (optional), styles, settings, outfit photos, captions, items, brands, prices. From usage: ratings, drip balance, style score, rank, statuses, follows, block list, invites, reports, support messages, technical events, IP address and browser type.

We do not receive your phone number, contacts or messages, do not collect location or advertising IDs and use no third-party analytics. Photos are not used to identify people and are not processed as biometric data.

## 3. Purposes and legal bases

Running the service, moderation, anti-fraud, support, notifications and bug fixing — based on the Terms and your consent. Showing your profile and looks to others — based on your separate consent to publication, where you choose which optional fields (gender, @username) are shown. No advertising use, no sale of data, no solely automated decisions with legal effect.

## 4. Recipients, storage and transfers

Data is collected and stored in databases located in the Russian Federation via [ЗАПОЛНИТЬ: российский хостинг-провайдер, ИНН], acting on our instructions. Vercel Inc. (USA) receives technical request data (IP, browser) to serve the interface — a cross-border transfer notified to Roskomnadzor. Telegram delivers notifications and support replies. Authorities receive data only on a lawful request.

## 5. Retention

Account data — until deletion or consent withdrawal, then deleted within 30 days. Deleted looks — removed from the feed at once, files within 30 days. Support messages and reports — 1 year. Technical events — 12 months, then only anonymised statistics.

## 6. Your rights

You may obtain information about processing and a copy of your data (Settings → My data), request correction, blocking or deletion, and withdraw either consent in whole or in part — via Settings → Support, @Driplycheckbot or [ЗАПОЛНИТЬ: e-mail]. We reply within 10 business days (extendable by 5 with reasons) and stop publishing data within 3 business days of a request. You may complain to Roskomnadzor or a court.

## 7. Minors and security

Driply is for users aged 14+; users aged 14–17 act with a guardian’s consent. Changes go through a server that verifies the Telegram signature; private fields are closed to the client; uploads use one-time signed links; only the controller has access. In case of a breach we notify Roskomnadzor within 24 hours, report results within 72 hours and inform affected users via the bot.
